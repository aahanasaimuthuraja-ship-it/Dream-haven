🧠 Sleep School

Learn about sleep

Quick, science-based facts to help you understand why sleep matters and how to build a healthier routine.

😴 How much sleep do teens need?

Most teenagers ages 13–18 should regularly get 8–10 hours of sleep per 24 hours.

🧠 Why is sleep important?

Sleep supports learning, memory, mood, attention, and normal growth and development.

📱 What about screens?

Bright light and engaging content can make it harder to wind down. Try giving yourself some screen-free time before bed.

⏰ A consistent schedule helps

Going to bed and waking up at roughly similar times each day can make it easier to get enough sleep.

🌙 A simple wind-down

Dim the lights, finish tasks, put your phone aside, and choose a calm activity such as reading or quiet music.

Educational information based on guidance from the CDC and American Academy of Sleep Medicine. Dream Haven is not medical advice.
